# QMS Record Templates

Document: REC-001. Revision/status/approval: see the [controlled document index](README.md#controlled-document-index).

Copy the relevant fields into a uniquely identified controlled record when the activity occurs. These templates are **blank forms**, not executed evidence. Keep `[PENDING]` where unknown. Every record needs ID, title, owner, date/time, state, applicable product/configuration/document revisions, evidence location/digest, linked records, reviewer, disposition and approval as appropriate. Preserve original results and later amendments separately under QMS-002.
