# Clinical Evaluation And Usability Plan

Document: EVAL-001. Revision/status/approval: see the [controlled document index](README.md#controlled-document-index).

## Objectives And Scope

Establish whether the exact released Neomate configuration is safe and performs as claimed for the approved neonatal population, professional users, workflows and environments. Class I still requires clinical evaluation. Source review, arithmetic verification, usability validation, and overall clinical evaluation are related but distinct evidence activities.

The clinical content lead owns the evaluation; appoint competent independent neonatal/pharmacy reviewers and an evaluator with relevant clinical-evaluation expertise. Retain competence and conflicts. The final feature list, precise patient limits, claims, outcome measures, evaluator appointments and protocol approvals are [PENDING].

## Evaluation Method

1. Freeze a claims inventory covering every calculation family, protocol, checklist and reference, including store descriptions and instructions. For each claim state intended users/patients, clinical task, expected benefit, measurable safety/performance outcome, required evidence, acceptance criteria and limitations. Do not infer benefits such as reduced medication errors without evidence.
2. Write a reproducible literature/source protocol before searching: questions, databases and authoritative source families, exact search terms, dates, inclusion/exclusion criteria, appraisal method, treatment of conflicting/negative evidence, and currency. Retain search results, decisions and licensed-source references in the approved store. Use the existing source register IDs.
3. Appraise clinical validity of each method/protocol, population and setting applicability, source quality and currency, competing guidance, limitations and clinical state of the art. Inherited behaviour and popularity of a comparator are not evidence of equivalence or correctness.
4. Independently derive expected calculation results from reviewed sources. Cover clinically meaningful limits, units/conversions, rounding, input errors, protocol constraints and failure/no-result states. Record source/version, derivation, tolerances, reviewer, executable vector and test result for the target revision.
5. Evaluate device-specific evidence: technical verification, representative clinician tasks, use-related risks, platform/offline limitations, and benefit-risk conclusions. Identify evidence gaps explicitly. Decide with the regulatory/clinical reviewers whether further data or a clinical investigation is required, including permissions and notifications before any patient-involving investigation.
6. Produce a clinical evaluation report with scope, methods, appraisals, favourable and unfavourable evidence, claim-by-claim conclusions, remaining gaps, risk/IFU implications, follow-up plan and signed reviewer conclusions. No such report is claimed by this plan.

## Usability Engineering

Create a use specification and critical-task inventory from neonatal workflows and HZ-001 to HZ-007. Include weight selection/change, decimal entry, mg/microgram and concentration interpretation, infusion preparation, result copying, warnings/advisories, source/review status, interruption/re-entry, and device/OS accessibility constraints. Specify realistic time pressure, lighting, gloves where relevant, text scaling, assistive technology, and screen size.

Run formative evaluations before design freeze. Then approve a validation protocol using representative users and realistic tasks on the claimed configurations. Set participant rationale, critical-task success criteria, permitted assistance, error/near-miss capture and analysis before running it. Accessibility checks and widget tests support, but do not replace, observed safe task performance. Retain findings, resulting changes and repeat-evaluation rationale. Use synthetic scenarios; no real patient data in the repository.

## Maintenance And Outputs

Review the clinical evaluation before each clinical release, at least annually as a proposed project cadence, and when claims, sources, users, risk or PMS change. Define post-market clinical follow-up activities or document an evidence-based justification for their extent; Class I alone is not a rationale to omit follow-up. Record outputs in TF-001 and feed conclusions to RISK-001, labelling and PMS-001.
