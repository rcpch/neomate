# Intended Purpose

## Proposed Intended Purpose

Neomate 2 is an offline-capable clinical calculator and guidance-reference application for appropriately trained neonatal healthcare professionals. It is intended to support neonatal care by presenting reviewed clinical reference material, checklists, and deterministic calculation support.

The initial platform target is Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, Linux, and web. Platform inclusion in an approved release is conditional on completing platform-specific verification, cybersecurity, usability, update, rollback, and clinical-safety release gates.

## Excluded Uses

Neomate 2 is not intended to diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, replace clinical judgement, replace local policy, replace independent calculation checks, store an electronic patient record, or process patient data unless a future controlled change defines and approves those functions.

## Current Status

The initial target market is the United Kingdom. The intended product is likely to require classification as a Class IIa medical device, but this is a planning assumption rather than a regulatory determination. The product is intended to be free to use. European Union and other market entry are deferred until after the UK route is established. Regulatory classification, clinical evaluation route, conformity assessment, and release labelling require formal assessment before deployment.
